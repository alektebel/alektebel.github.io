the part that generalises

Our LLM system at the bank was generating the right content, but the client required it in their own approved Word templates, and no existing tool could fill them. I went down to the raw XML and built a library that clones, fills and duplicates table fragments from the approved template on the fly, with heuristic fallbacks for tables it had never seen. It's now in production across three languages, used in every audit project for the entity.

The bottleneck for AI in regulated industries often isn't the model or the content: it's getting the output into the client's final deliverable.
